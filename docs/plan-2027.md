# The 2027 season plan (approved by the user 2026-11-18; items 5–7 below still in progress)

One place for what gets frozen before the 2027 season, what is recorded during it, and what is judged after it.
It collects decisions already made elsewhere (`docs/model-tests.md`, `docs/edge-plan-2026-11-16.md`, `CLAUDE.md`) and
adds the few that were still open. Where this file and an earlier pre-registration differ, **the pre-registration
wins**: nothing here loosens a rule that was written before its data.

**Why 2027 matters.** About 50 model variants have been tried on 2018–25, and 2026 is partly used up. The 2027 season,
with the model frozen before week 0, is the first clean test of whether any of this beats the market. Every judgement
below is about that record.

## 2. The frozen model

### 2.1 What goes in
- **v5** (as live today).
- **T1, full matchup rows**: passed Rule C on 2026-11-17 (edge test).
- **T-OCDC, new offensive / defensive coordinator**: passed the accuracy rule on 2026-11-18; included by the user's
  decision the same day. Reads table `coordinators`.
- **Coach career** (learned feature, Test 6 definition) **only if Holdout 5A is "Confirmed"** on Dec 7. If 5A is
  "Consistent", it is tracked as a paper model instead (section 4). If "Rejected", it's dropped.
- Nothing else. Every other idea is either rejected or a paper hypothesis (section 6).

### 2.2 The no-harm check (once, in August, before the freeze)
The stack has never been run together. Each part passed against v5 alone, so the combination gets one check on
2021–25, with the same items as the accuracy rule's no-harm part (no new judged test, no tuning):
- spread MAE better than v5 (pooled);
- Δ result slope at the Bovada open ≥ −1 paired SE; ATS 3+ at the open not down by more than 1.0 pp;
  CLV % not down by more than 1.0 pp; CLV points not down by more than 2 paired SE.
- **Reported, not judged (added 2026-11-18):** calibration by weeks played (0–2, 3–4, 5–9, 10+) for the full frozen
  stack, beside v5's (v5 + T1 is 0.85–0.87 in weeks 3–9 on 2021–25; plan-2026-11-18.md D-EARLY).

**If it fails (order fixed now, approved 2026-11-18):** drop T-OCDC (accuracy-only) and re-check once; if that fails, drop coach
career and re-check once; T1 stays in every version, since it is the only part that passed an edge rule. If v5 + T1
alone fails, freeze v5 unchanged. Every re-check is reported, and no part is ever re-tuned.

### 2.3 Data upkeep before the freeze (each August, already listed in `CLAUDE.md`)
- Backfill the new season: `python -m cfb.backfill 2027`, then `python -m cfb.load 2027` (~11 CFBD calls; check
  `/info` first).
- `load_coach_changes(last_season=2026)` once CFBD has filled 2026's `/coaches`.
- Coordinators: loaded automatically by the coordinator detector (Mondays; or run `python -m cfb.coordinators 2027`
  by hand); check the 2027 rows by hand before the freeze, since `is_new` feeds the frozen model.
- NFL: check the first 2027 nflverse load and the first NFL odds snapshot.

### 2.4 Freezing
- **Version name:** `v6` (college). Its live predictions are stored as `model_version = 'v6'`, never replayed (the
  backtest guard protects them).
- **Code:** a git tag on the commit that produces `v6`, made the day of the freeze. Any later change to the files it
  depends on (`cfb/model.py`, `cfb/ratings.py`, `cfb/priors.py`, `cfb/picks.py`, the coordinator and coach loaders)
  must not change `v6`'s output; if it would, it waits for 2028.
- **Settings:** pick rules (Play 3, totals 3, moved 0.5 unless changed before the freeze with a logged reason) and the
  odds snapshot schedule are fixed for the season. The Results page change log is the check that they stayed fixed.
- **Training:** 2017–2026 (2020 handled as today), points model refit once at the freeze, not during the season.

## 3. Betting in 2027

- **What counts as "the bet":** the pick recorded at bet time (019 rule: first snapshot with 4+ books, 8+ hours after
  both teams' previous kickoff), at the **bettable open** (best number among the bettable books in that snapshot).
- **Which picks:** Play tier only (3+ off the market and not moved against us). Caution and Pass are recorded, not bet.
- **Season phase:** if **Test B** is "supported" on Dec 7, no real bets before weeks-played 5 (picks still recorded).
  If not, bets from week 0.
- **Power-conference tier:** if **Holdout T** is "supported", tracked as a tag only; no change to which picks are bet.
- **Stakes:** a fixed unit per Play (amount: your decision, section 7). Real bets go in the bets log; its record at the
  prices actually taken is the real ROI that rule 5C uses.
- **Stopping:** rule 5C (model-tests.md section 5C) unchanged: circuit breaker after 200 Plays (mean CLV points ≤ 0 →
  stop), then continue / stop / scale back after the season by its written thresholds.
- **NFL:** no real bets. Paper picks at the Monday 6:00 AM MT bet time (T-OPEN's consequence), as built on
  2026-11-17.

## 5. During the season: what is and isn't allowed

- **Allowed:** overrides (injuries, QB news, weather) entered before kickoff, as now; they're logged and shown. Hand
  entries of mid-season coach changes (`coach_changes`) and coordinator changes (`coordinators`, source `manual`).
  Bug fixes that don't change `v6`'s numbers. Pages, the bets log, the admin page.
- **Not allowed:** any change to `v6`'s code, features or parameters; pick-rule or snapshot-schedule changes; new tests
  judged on 2027 games before the season ends; looking at 2027 results by any cut not listed in section 6.
- If a change is unavoidable (e.g. a source breaks), it's logged with a reason and the date, and the judged runs
  report results before and after it.

### Display note for the predictions page (added 2026-11-18; not a rule change)
In weeks played 3–9 the frozen stack runs about 10–15% steep (v5 + T1 calibration 0.85–0.87 on 2021–25,
plan-2026-11-18.md D-EARLY): a 3-point gap there is worth roughly 2.6–2.7 points. Show this on the page as a muted note

picks use the gap as computed.

weeks 3–4, 0.872 for 5–9); nothing shows for v5. Freeze-runbook step: replace the factors with `freeze_check.py`'s own v6
calibration.

### Reported measure for the 2027 spread record (added 2026-11-18; changes no rule)
Beside the Bovada-based line move (Bovada open → median close), report the **line move from the bet-time median of our
books to the last pre-kickoff median** (the measure H-TOT uses), on all Plays and as a slope on our gap at bet time. This
is the stronger opener-noise check for spreads: the college B1 benchmark was weaker than the opener (B1-spreads,
plan-2026-11-18.md), so a median-to-median move toward us in 2027 is the real test of whether the spread signal is more
than Bovada-opener noise.

## 8. Challengers (paper models), registered 2026-11-19 (user), before any 2027 game

### 8.1 Shared rules
- **Champions:** `v6` (college; the only model that drives real picks) and `nfl-v3` (NFL; paper picks only).
- **Registered before 2027 week 0** with its exact definition, the comparison with the champion, the judged metric and
  the bar. Nothing is added, removed or promoted during the season. Each judged item is judged once, after the regular
  season (after the 2028 regular season for two-season items).
- **Same inputs:** overrides apply to **none** of the models in challenger comparisons. The champion is compared on its
  own raw line, saved each morning in `paper_predictions` beside the challengers (college and NFL: built 2026-11-19). Real picks still use v6 + overrides, and `pick_log` keeps that record.
  Overrides are hand entries made with the line in view; keeping them out keeps every comparison model-against-model.
- **Bet time is the champion's** (college: first 4+-book snapshot 8+ h after both teams' previous kickoff; NFL: Monday
  6:00 AM MT). A model's line at bet time = its latest `paper_predictions` row created before that snapshot.
- **Two kinds:** (a) a separate model with a daily run (`paper.py`); (b) a formula on stored outputs (no runner; only
  the formula is registered, and it is computed once at judging with the frozen code).
- **Monitoring:** every type (a) model is in `paper.all_versions()`, so `paper_check` and `/metrics` cover it. Type (b)
  writes no rows; its inputs are covered.
- **Bars:** one family per league, one-sided Bonferroni at 5% over that league's judged items (k items → bar
  z = Φ⁻¹(1 − 0.05/k)): 1 → 1.645, 2 → 1.96, 3 → 2.13, 4 → 2.24, 5 → 2.33, 6 → 2.39, 7 → 2.45, 8 → 2.50.
- **Power, measured on stored backtest rows (SEs only):** one-season paired SE grows with the SD of the line difference
  between the two models. College (~850 games), per 1 pt of that SD: line-move slope ≈ 0.0025–0.004, result slope at
  the open ≈ 0.018, MAE ≈ 0.03–0.04. NFL (~270 games, nfl-v3 vs nfl-v2, SD 1.7): line move 0.015–0.020, result slope
  at Monday 0.10–0.23, MAE 0.08–0.12.

### 9.1 Both leagues: a reversal check beside every move-based result (reported, not judged)
Moskowitz (2021) finds that about half of an average NFL open → close move is reversed by the outcome (coefficient
≈ −0.50). Line-move and result scorecards therefore stay separate (literature.md 3.2):
- (i) line move: line-move slope and CLV in points;
- (ii) result at bet time at the bettable number;
- (iii) **reversal check:** result slope at the last pre-kickoff median, plus, on our Plays / 3+ gaps, the regression
  of the (last median → result) return on the (bet-time median → last median) return.

(iii) near 0 means the moves toward us are information; near −0.5 means they are ordinary market moves that reverse.
A pass on (i) alone never promotes anything to betting. Code: two measures in `report_2027.py`, both leagues (user
types it).
