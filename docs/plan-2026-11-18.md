### B1-spreads and B1-totals: is our line move more than opener noise? (DRAFT, to approve; runs first)
**Question.** College has never had the control NFL got in T-OPEN-B. If the Bovada opener is noisy, any sensible line
built without that noise "sees the line move toward it". B1 asks whether v5's gap still predicts the move once the
market's own earlier view is in the regression.

**Benchmark B1 (fixed now), built only from earlier closing lines:**
- *Closing line used:* the median close of the CFBD books (Bovada, DraftKings, ESPN Bet, William Hill NJ, Caesars),
  Bovada's close where the median is missing; FBS-involved games only (no closes exist for the rest).
- *Spreads:* least squares of closing home spreads on team ratings plus home field:
  close = R_away − R_home − H·(not neutral); ridge α = 1 (`cfb/ratings.py` ALPHA), one rating per team (FBS and FCS).
- *Totals:* closing totals on team total-ratings plus an intercept: close_total = T_home + T_away + c; ridge α = 1.
- *Games used and weights:* for a game in week block W of season S, only games that kicked off before W's first
  kickoff (v5's rating cutoff, which is before the Sunday Bovada open), weighted exactly as v5's ratings: this season by
  recency (half-life RECENCY_HALF_LIFE = 4 weeks of weeks played), last season at weight 0.5^(OFFSEASON_AGE_WEEKS / 4)
  fading to 0 over PRIOR_WEEKS = 6. Week-0/1 blocks use last season only. No play counts (closing lines have none).
- *Prediction:* spread B1 = R_away − R_home − H (H = 0 at neutral sites); total B1_t = T_home + T_away + c.
- B1 never sees our model, the opener, results or anything after the cutoff.

**Games.** The Rule C set: 2021–25, FBS-involved, completed, Bovada open + median close (spreads n = 4,169; totals the
4,164 with a Bovada open total and a median close total). Games without a B1 (a team with no earlier closing line in
the window) are left out and counted.

**Main tests (one regression each, season-week bootstrap 2,000):**
- *B1-spreads:* move = a·(ours − open) + b·(B1 − open) + c, move = median close − Bovada open.
- *B1-totals:* the same with totals (our total, B1_t, Bovada open total, median close total).
- **Supported** if a ≥ **2.24 × bootstrap SE** above zero (bar from the count below).

**Reported (not judged):** b and c; B1's own line move on its 3+ gaps beside v5's (+0.56 spreads); how often B1 and v5
pick the same side on v5's 3+ gaps; a by season; the same regression with result − open as the outcome; B1's MAE
against the open, the close and results.

**Then (user, 2026-11-18):** spreads supported → nothing changes; spreads not supported → flagged in CLAUDE.md as the
biggest open question before 2027. Totals supported → the totals paper tier (below) is registered; totals not
supported → totals stay info only and the tier is not registered. No variants: no other benchmark, weights, books or
alpha.

### SEL-v0: bet-selection layer, first version (DRAFT, revised; judged on 2024–25)
- **Seen before drafting:** D-SEL's one-at-a-time cuts on 2021–25 (hindsight). The inputs were named in the review
  request and the edge plan before D-SEL ran; none is added or dropped because of D-SEL.
- **Rows:** v5's 3+ gaps at the Bovada open, 2021–25 FBS-involved. **Target:** move toward our pick =
  sign(our line − open) × (median close − Bovada open).
- **Full model (inputs fixed):** |gap|; Test L's luck aligned with our pick (`x_pick`) + "no X" indicator (X set to 0);
  weeks-played-0–4 indicator; both-teams-power-4 indicator; DraftKings lookahead gap aligned with our pick (`dk_pick`) +
  "no DraftKings open" indicator (dk_pick set to 0; covers all of 2021–22). Plain OLS, no interactions, no tuning.
- **Baseline:** the same OLS with |gap| only. Both fit once on 2021–23.
- **Main test:** on 2024–25, Δr = corr(full prediction, actual move) − corr(gap-only prediction, actual move), paired
  season-week bootstrap; **pass if Δr ≥ 2.24 × its SE.**
  - *Why correlation, not R²:* the layer's job is to rank picks (bet the higher predicted moves). Correlation measures
    ranking information and ignores a season-wide shift in the average move size between the fit and judged seasons
    (the line-move slope was +0.159 in 2025 vs +0.07–0.10 before), which would lower R² for reasons that have nothing
    to do with the inputs. The gap-only model's correlation is simply corr(|gap|, move), so Δr is the inputs' added
    ranking value.
- **Reported, not judged:** top vs bottom half of the full model's predicted move on 2024–25: ATS at the Bovada open
  and CLV points (each half n ≈ 420, ATS about ±4.7 pp at 95%); the same for the gap-only model; coefficients.
- **Then:** pass → paper-tracked in 2027 beside the Play tier (never changes which picks are bet); fail → dropped.
