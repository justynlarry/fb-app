# Where else edge could come from: findings and plan, 2026-11-16

Written after the NFL audit (`docs/nfl-audit-2026-11-16.md`) and about 45 model tests across both leagues.
Part 1 is what a set of read-only checks showed. Part 2 is the plan for three free pieces of work, with the rules
written before anything is run. **Nothing in Part 2 has been run.**

All college numbers use the stored v5 backtest, 2021–25, graded at the Bovada open with Bovada's own close
(n = 4,169). 2026 was not looked at. The cuts in Part 1 are hindsight: about 15 were made, so each is a hypothesis.

Scripts: scratch `ideas.py` (session 2026-11-16).

## Part 1. Findings

### 1.1 The model predicts line movement far more clearly than it predicts results
Same games, same gaps (our line − the open), two outcomes:

| Outcome regressed on our gap | Slope | In SEs |
|---|---|---|
| Result − open | +0.113 ± 0.047 | 2.4 |
| Close − open ("line-move slope") | +0.096 ± 0.017 | 5.6 |

- A game result is noisy: SD of (result − open) is 15.4 points. A line move is not: SD of (close − open) is 2.2.
  So the line-move slope is measured almost three times as precisely.
- It is positive in all five seasons: +0.056, +0.051, +0.224, +0.058, +0.063.
- What is left at the close is about zero (+0.016). The two slopes agree, as they should if the close is efficient.
- By gap size, the line moves toward us by +0.36 points (gaps of 3–5), +0.58 (5–7), +0.73 (7–10), +1.03 (10+).

**Meaning.** Our edge is that the opener moves toward our number. We have been judging every idea on results,
the noisiest possible outcome.

### 1.2 That edge alone does not beat −110
- On 3+ picks the line moves 0.56 ± 0.10 points our way.
- A point near the open is worth about 2.8 points of win rate (16.9% of results land within 3 of the open).
- 0.56 points ≈ 51.6%. Break-even is 52.4% at −110, about 51.5% at LowVig's prices. Measured: 52.4% ± 2.1.

### 1.3 Books disagree by enough to matter (our snapshots: 9 snapshots, 127 games, four days)
- The best number is on average 0.39 points better than the median, for whichever side you want. It is 0.5+
  better 56% of the time and 1.0+ better 13%.
- Off the median by 0.5+ most often: BetRivers (48%), FanDuel (44%), Bovada (30%). Others 14–22%.
- Vig on spreads: LowVig 2.9%, BetMGM 4.3%, most others 4.7%, BetRivers 5.5%.
- Model move (0.56) + best number (0.39) ≈ 0.95 points ≈ 52.7%. Thin, but the first arithmetic that is positive.

### 1.4 Knowing which gaps the market will agree with is worth far more than a better rating
3+ picks, graded at the open, split by what the line did afterwards (hindsight):

| Afterwards the line… | n | Win rate at the open |
|---|---|---|
| moved 1+ our way | 836 | 57.2% |
| moved less than 1 | 774 | 51.7% |
| moved 1+ against us | 484 | 45.2% |

### 1.5 Season phase and conference tier (hypotheses)
| Games | n | Line-move slope | Result slope | 3+ picks: move, win |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks played 0–4 | 1,570 | +0.057 ± 0.009 | +0.003 ± 0.067 | +0.50, 49.9% |
| Weeks played 5+ | 2,599 | +0.130 ± 0.028 | +0.210 ± 0.068 | +0.61, 54.1% |
| Both teams in the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12 or ACC | 1,674 | +0.140 ± 0.020 | +0.322 ± 0.098 | +0.72, 52.6% |
| All other games | 2,495 | +0.081 ± 0.021 | +0.044 ± 0.055 | +0.48, 52.3% |

- Phase: the line-move slope is higher after week 5 (difference about 2.5 SE), but in points the move is similar
  (0.50 vs 0.61). The 49.9% vs 54.1% win-rate gap is bigger than the line movement explains, so part of it is luck.
- Tier: slopes are higher in power-conference games (line-move difference +0.058 ± 0.028, result difference
  +0.278 ± 0.114), but the win rates on 3+ picks are the same (52.6% vs 52.3%). Weak.
- Conference membership is today's, so teams that changed conference are labelled by where they are now.
  **Correction:** the first version of this cut counted today's Pac-12 (eight former Mountain West-level teams)
  as a power conference. The table above uses the four conferences only.

### 1.8 Cached or free data the models don't use
- College, already cached (0 calls): line yards, stuff rate, power success, second-level and open-field yards,
  standard-down and passing-down splits, rushing and passing success rates.
- College play-by-play (sportsdataverse): special teams plays, turnover detail, penalties.
- NFL (nflverse; files confirmed to exist): player pay (a talent measure for every position), depth charts,
  Next Gen Stats, PFR advanced stats, officials.
- Expectation from 45 tests: these make a model more accurate, not more different from the market.

## Part 2. Plan (not run; needs approval)

**Decision recorded (user, 2026-11-16):** a bet-selection layer that uses market data is allowed, kept separate
from the rating model. The rating model's rule is unchanged: market lines are never its inputs.

Shared rules: college, v5, 2021–25, Bovada open and Bovada close, FBS-involved games with both numbers. Bootstrap
of games, 2,000 resamples, paired where two models are compared. Bar 2.24 SE. No variants after results. A pass
makes a candidate for the frozen 2027 record, never an adoption for betting.

### Step 1. Rule C: line movement as the first screen for new ideas
- **Metric:** line-move slope = slope of (close − open) on (our line − open).
- **Rule C, for any later model change:** pass if the paired change in line-move slope is ≥ 2.24 paired SE,
  **and** the paired change in result slope is not negative, **and** the slope left at the close does not rise
  by more than 2 paired SE (a change must work through the line moving, not by disagreeing with the close),
  **and** spread MAE is not worse. Rule B stays as registered for anything already registered under it.
- **Run now (information only, 0 new models):** score every stored version on both slopes: v1, v3, v4, v5, and
  the saved variant rows for coach career (Test 4 / Test 6) and drive metrics (Round 4), where the files still
  exist. Report each paired difference from v5.
- **What it can and can't do:** it can't adopt or reject anything. Re-scoring about ten old variants with a new
  metric would pass one by luck about 12% of the time at 2.24. Anything that clears is a hypothesis for 2027.
  Holdout 5A (December 7) is unchanged and is not looked at.
- **Sanity check, written now:** across v1 → v5 the two slopes should rise together. If they don't, the metric is
  not measuring the same thing as results and Rule C is dropped.

### Step 2. Test L: does the opener overreact to luck in last week's score?
- **Idea.** Openers react to final scores. Play-level numbers say who deserved to win. A team that won by more
  than it deserved should be overvalued in next week's opener.
- **Deserved margin.** For season S, a linear regression of final margin on the game's own stat differences
  (EPA per play, success rate, explosiveness, plays; offense minus the opponent's, from `team_game_stats`), fitted
  on FBS-vs-FBS games of seasons before S. Luck = actual margin − deserved margin, for the team.
- **Input for a game.** X = home team's luck in its previous game − away team's luck in its previous game. Both
  teams must have played an FBS opponent earlier in the same season, within the last 15 days.
- **Outcomes, in home-margin terms.** Line move = how far the market's home margin rose from open to close.
  Result = home margin − the open's home margin.
- **Supported** if the slope of line move on X is ≤ −2.24 SE (the market takes back part of the reaction during
  the week) **and** the slope of result on X is negative.
- **Reported:** both slopes; the same with v5's gap in the regression (is this already inside our model?);
  X in thirds with cover rates at the open.
- **Then:** supported → an input for the bet-selection layer, tracked in 2027. Not supported → dropped.
- One model (the deserved-margin regression). 0 calls.

### Step 3. Holdout T: conference tier, added to Test B on December 7
- Test B (season phase) stays exactly as registered in model-tests.md section 8.
- **Holdout T**, same games and date as Test B (2026 through December 6, Bovada open): *supported* if the line-move
  slope for games with both teams in the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12 or ACC is higher than for all other games, **and**
  the result slope is too. Not supported → dropped. Supported → tracked in 2027; pre-registration C judges it.
- Reported beside both B and T, not part of either rule: the line-move slopes by phase.
- One season cannot confirm either (about 300 power-conference games; the line-move slope's SE will be about 0.05).
- 2026 has not been looked at by conference tier. It has been looked at by month, by FCS opponent and for
  weeknight Group of 5 games.
- No model.

### Count and false-pass risk
One judged test at 2.24 SE (Test L): about 1.25%. Holdout T and Test B are direction-only rules on one season:
each is "supported" by luck roughly a quarter of the time, which is why neither can do more than earn a place
in the 2027 record. Step 1 judges nothing.

### Not in this plan
- **The bet-selection layer itself.** It needs timed lines from several books: either a season of our own
  snapshots or bought history. To be registered when the data question is settled. Inputs in mind: our gap, where
  the lower-vig books sit against the median, movement in the first hours, Test L's input if supported.
- **NFL opener tests** (T-OPEN, T-LOOK): same data question. A free, weaker version is possible from ESPN's
  open and close for 2024–25 (one book, no times).
- **NFL teasers:** needs a log of the prices actually offered before it is worth anything.
- **Unused cached college stats:** one batch under Rule C, after Step 1 shows Rule C behaves.
