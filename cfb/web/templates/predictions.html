"52% in backtest (1,115–1,017)" is how that kind of pick has done in our backtest (2021–25), with its win–loss record (pushes left out): Play and Pass are graded at Bovada's opening line (when we'd bet), Caution and totals at the closing line. On finished games, ✓ right side / ✗ wrong side grades a side we didn't record as a pick (a Pass lean, or a game before Nov 12) against the closing line; recorded picks keep their W / L with units. Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. Line chips: ▲ our way the line has moved toward our number since it opened, ▼ against away from it. Our edge has been biggest at the opening line, so the earlier in the week, the better. The open is Bovada's opening line.
