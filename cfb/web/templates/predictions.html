we differ from the market by 3+ points 5+ points. "Our edge" names the team we rate higher than the market does. Large gaps are worth checking for injuries or news the model can't see.

Open is the average opening line across books ("best" = the best opening number for the side we like). Market now shows how far the line has moved since: our way or against us. In our backtest we've done best when we disagree with the opening line; a gap that's still there at kickoff, after the market has moved, has been a losing bet.

Weather: forecast at kickoff (Open-Meteo, updated each morning). ⚠ = gusts 25+ mph or 0.1+ in. of rain. Our model doesn't use weather and has tended to over-predict totals in games like these; the market usually prices it in. Worth a look on the Overrides page.
