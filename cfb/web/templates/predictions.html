Pass under 3 points, including when the market has already moved to our number. Info totals 3+ apart; for display only, our totals haven't beaten the market. Under 3 apart, a grey "O/U lean" line shows which way our total leans (not recorded or graded).

"Now" is the median of every sportsbook's current line (The Odds API, updated each morning, Sunday afternoon and evening, and Saturday before kickoff). Play and Caution show the best number available for our side, then the best price, and the book that has it. "52% in backtest (1,115–1,017)" is how that kind of pick has done in our backtest (2021–25), with its win–loss record (pushes left out): Play and Pass are graded at Bovada's opening line (when we'd bet), Caution and totals at the closing line. On finished games, ✓ right side / ✗ wrong side grades a side we didn't record as a pick (a Pass lean, or a game before Nov 12) against the closing line; recorded picks keep their W / L with units. Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. Line chips: ▲ our way the line has moved toward our number since it opened, ▼ against away from it. Our edge has been biggest at the opening line, so the earlier in the week, the better. The open is Bovada's opening line.

Weather: forecast at kickoff (Open-Meteo, updated each morning). ⚠ = gusts 25+ mph or 0.1+ in. of rain. Our model doesn't use weather and has tended to over-predict totals in games like these; the market usually prices it in. Worth a look on the Overrides page.
