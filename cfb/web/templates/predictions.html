"Now" is the median of every sportsbook's current line (The Odds API, updated each morning, Sunday afternoon and evening, and Saturday before kickoff). Play and Caution show the best number available for our side, then the best price, and the book that has it. The % is how that kind of pick has done in our backtest (2021–25, graded at the closing line), out of that many past games. Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. Line chips: ▲ our way the line has moved toward our number since it opened, ▼ against away from it. Our edge has been biggest at the opening line, so the earlier in the week, the better; "best open" is the best opening number across books.

Weather: forecast at kickoff (Open-Meteo, updated each morning). ⚠ = gusts 25+ mph or 0.1+ in. of rain. Our model doesn't use weather and has tended to over-predict totals in games like these; the market usually prices it in. Worth a look on the Overrides page.
