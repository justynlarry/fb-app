Summary excludes 2020 (no crowds). The NFL "market" is nflverse's one line per game: roughly the close, with no book or time, and its source changes in 2023 and 2025. There's no NFL opening line in this data, so there are no line-moved or at-the-open columns, and these results measure accuracy at the close, not betting edge. The Monday-line tests (T-OPEN, T-OPEN-B) used separate line files; see docs/model-tests.md section 9.

{%- else %}

Summary excludes 2020 (COVID season). "Line moved toward us" = closing line value: when we disagreed with the opening line, how often the market moved our way by kickoff. "At the open" grades the side we'd have taken against Bovada's opening line, at that line, and line movement is Bovada's open to Bovada's close. Only Bovada is used because it posts a true Sunday opener; other books' "open" is often a lookahead line from before the previous week's games. Opening lines are only available from 2021.
