Break-even at −110 is 52.4% (win % in green when above it). ±95% is the margin of error on the win rate: with this few picks, anything inside it can't be told apart from a coin flip. Pass rows grade the side we leaned to, for comparison; they aren't bets.

{%- if is_nfl %}

Which line? Each NFL pick is graded at the best number and price across books at the moment we saved it. NFL lines are saved every morning at 6 AM Mountain (after the 5:30 AM NFL update), plus the Sunday and Saturday snapshots. "Early" is the Monday pick: the first save with 4+ books on Monday of game week, 6 AM to 6 AM Tuesday Mountain (the bet time tested in T-OPEN). A game with no Monday save has no Early pick; a later save doesn't stand in for it. Week 11 of 2026 has none (saving started Wednesday, Nov 18). "Late" is the last save before kickoff. There's no NFL opening line in our data, so there's no "True open" column and no Caution tier (Caution needs the line's move since the open). Every 3+ gap is a Play.

{%- else %}

Which line? Neither column is exactly the market open or close. Each pick is graded at the best number and price across books at the moment we saved it. Lines are saved every morning at 6 AM, Sunday at 4 and 8 PM (soon after openers are posted), and Saturday at 9 AM and noon (Mountain). "Early" is the pick at bet time: the first save with 4+ books that comes after both teams' previous games, usually Sunday at 4 PM (week 11: Thursday, Nov 12, when tracking began). Earlier lookahead lines from one or two books are saved but not graded. "Late" is the last save before kickoff: noon Saturday for afternoon and night games, 6 AM the same day for weeknight games, so it can be several hours before the true close. "True open" grades our first saved line against Bovada's opening line (from CFBD) at an assumed −110. Only Bovada is used: other books' "open" is often a lookahead line from the week before. At the open there's no line movement yet, so every 3+ gap is a Play (no Caution row) and the rest are Pass.
