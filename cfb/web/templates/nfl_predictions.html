"52% in backtest (1,115–1,017)" is how that kind of game went in our NFL backtest (2021–25), graded at the closing line, with its win–loss record (pushes left out). Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. The market line is nflverse's (one consensus number, no prices or books).

Once a game starts, the Pick column shows the paper pick saved before kickoff instead: the best number and price across the books in our odds feed, graded W/L with units (risking 1). These picks are a record for testing, not bets; the full record is on the Results page. Games before the first save (Nov 18, 2026) are checked against the nflverse line, without units. ✓ right side / ✗ wrong side grades a Pass lean (not a pick) against the same line.
