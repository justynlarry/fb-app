Pick rules

These decide which picks are recorded. A change applies from the next odds snapshot on: picks already in the log keep the tier they had. Every change is logged with your reason and listed with its date, so the record shows which rule each pick was made under. The backtest rates on the Predictions page are recomputed for the new numbers right away. For the 2027 test, leave these alone once the season starts.

Defaults: Play 3, totals 3, moved 0.5 (the rules every backtest so far was judged under).

Schedule

When the worker runs each job (Mountain time, 24-hour clock). It re-reads this every 5 minutes, so a saved change takes effect within 5 minutes, with no restart. Changing the odds snapshot times needs a reason and is listed on the Results page, because they decide which lines picks are recorded at.

Defaults: college update 05:00 (retries 05:30, 07:30), NFL update 05:30, odds snapshots daily 06:00 · Sat 09:00, 12:00 · Sun 16:00, 20:00 (about 189 Odds API credits a month in season); polls 30 s / 600 s.

Bettable books

Books you can actually bet at. They're suggested in the Bet form's Book field, and only these are prefilled from a pick's best price. Every other book in the odds feed (Bovada, BetOnline…) stays a reference line. This doesn't change how picks are priced or recorded.
